Primary request: convincing deep space seen by an astronaut, a rich but elegant astronomical star field with delicate dusty violet-blue emission nebulae and subtle warm rose clouds, dense tiny pinprick stars of varied brightness and color temperature, dark interstellar dust filaments, very fine detail, realistic volumetric nebula texture, cinematic natural space photography aesthetic. Composition: wide 3:2 panoramic environment, dark navy-black negative space around center for the tunnel vanishing point and readable website text, brighter detailed wisps along upper left and far right; NO planets, NO rings, NO meteors, NO tunnel or built objects, NO people, NO text, NO logos. These objects will be added as animated layers in code. Avoid flat painted shapes, cartoon, overexposed white core, lens flare streaks. Output a high-quality landscape raster background.
